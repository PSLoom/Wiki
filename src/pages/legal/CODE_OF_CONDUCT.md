# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

PSLoom welcomes everyone, regardless of background, identity, disability or experience. Participation should be respectful and free from harassment.

## Community standards

- Be considerate, listen to different perspectives and offer constructive feedback.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and their impact.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, make personal attacks or direct unwanted sexual attention at others.
- Do not disclose another person's private information without permission.

## Scope

These standards apply to PSLoom repositories, discussions and other project spaces, and when officially representing PSLoom elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to [psloom@baliestri.dev](mailto:psloom@baliestri.dev). Include relevant context and links where possible. Avoid posting sensitive reports in public issues.

Maintainers review reports fairly and protect reporters' privacy. Retaliation is unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove contributions or restrict participation. Depending on severity and repetition, responses include a private correction, a warning, a temporary restriction or permanent exclusion. Serious incidents may warrant immediate exclusion. Maintainers explain decisions when appropriate.
