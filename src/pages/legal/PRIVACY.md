# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This policy covers the PSLoom documentation website at
[psloom.github.io/wiki](https://psloom.github.io/wiki/), maintained by the PSLoom project.
It describes the website's current configuration. It does not describe data stored
locally by PowerShell modules you install or run.

For privacy questions or requests, contact
[psloom@baliestri.dev](mailto:psloom@baliestri.dev).

## Reading the documentation

The wiki is a static website. Reading it does not require an account. The site
does not provide registration, payment forms or newsletter subscriptions.
Its current configuration includes no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking
pixels added by PSLoom.

Images, styles, scripts and the search index are served with the website.

## Hosting

The website is configured for GitHub Pages. GitHub processes information needed
to deliver and secure the site. In particular, GitHub states that it logs visitors'
IP addresses for security, whether or not they are signed in.

See [GitHub Pages data collection](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)
and the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)
for GitHub's processing, retention and privacy request procedures.

## Browser storage and search

The site uses browser storage to remember interface preferences, such as your
choice of light or dark theme. You can remove these preferences by clearing site
data in your browser. The site may also follow your operating system's color preference.

Documentation search runs in your browser using an index downloaded from the
website. The site does not submit your search terms to a hosted search provider.
Search terms may appear in the address bar when opening search results; URLs
can be stored in browser history and included when you share a link.

PSLoom does not add advertising or analytics cookies to the wiki. Browser storage
for interface preferences is separate from the hosting provider's processing.

## Messages and community contributions

If you email us, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose
to send. We use that information to respond to your request or handle a community
report. Email providers also process messages as part of delivering and storing them.
Please send only the personal information needed for your request.

Access to private reports should be limited to those handling them. Information
may need to be shared to investigate an incident, protect participants or comply
with a legal obligation. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the
request, follow up on an incident or meet applicable obligations.

Issues, pull requests and other contributions made in public GitHub spaces are
public. Avoid including private information in them. GitHub handles these
contributions under its own policies; repository history, forks and other copies
can preserve published material.

## Your choices and requests

You can clear browser storage and history using your browser settings. Contact
[psloom@baliestri.dev](mailto:psloom@baliestri.dev) to ask about information you have
provided to the project or request access, correction or deletion. Available
rights and exceptions depend on applicable law. We may need to confirm your
identity before acting on a request.

Requests concerning information held independently by GitHub should follow the
procedures in its privacy statement. The project cannot erase copies controlled
by other parties.

## External links

Following a link to GitHub or another external website takes you to a service
with its own privacy practices. Review that service's policy before providing
personal information.

## Changes

When the site's features or practices change, we will update this page and its
revision date. The current policy remains available through the wiki's footer.
